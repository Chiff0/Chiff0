### Filip Dobnikar

I write software that sits close to the hardware.

Right now that's my bachelor's thesis: a YOLO inference engine written from scratch in C++, CUDA and PTX for the Jetson Orin Nano. It uses no frameworks. It goes up against TensorRT, ONNX Runtime and Darknet, and gets judged on accuracy per watt, read straight off the board's power monitor.

Final year of CS at the University of Ljubljana, in the [ViCoS](https://www.vicos.si) lab. Next: MSc Computer & Embedded Systems Engineering at TU Delft.
